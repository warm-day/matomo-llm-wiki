## Data Privacy
- **1st Party Cookies**: Matomo uses 1st party cookies for tracking purposes (source: Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md).
- **GDPR Compliance**: Matomo provides tools to help users exercise their GDPR rights, including access, erasure, and objection (source: Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md). See [[gdpr-compliance]].
- **Consent Management**: Matomo supports two levels of consent to balance privacy with data accuracy. See [[consent-management]].
- **Cookieless Tracking**: Matomo can be configured to track visitors without cookies, potentially removing the need for consent banners. See [[cookieless-tracking]].
- **Cookie Documentation**: For a comprehensive list of Matomo cookies, their specific purposes, and expiration times, refer to the official [Matomo FAQ 146](https://matomo.org/faq/general/faq_146/).

## Operational Privacy Procedures
The following procedures must be followed to ensure privacy compliance (source: Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md):

### Matomo Cloud
- **Privacy Notice**: Inform visitors through a clear [privacy notice](https://matomo.org/blog/2018/04/how-should-i-write-my-privacy-notice-for-matomo-analytics-under-gdpr/) whenever collecting personal data.
- **Privacy Policy**: Inform users in your [privacy policy](https://matomo.org/blog/2018/04/how-to-complete-your-privacy-policy-with-matomo-analytics-under-gdpr/) about what data you collect and how it is used.
- **Team Awareness**: Ensure the team is aware of [what data is being collected](https://matomo.org/faq/general/faq_18254/) by the analytics platform.
- **Information Asset Register**: Document Matomo usage within your [information asset register](https://matomo.org/blog/2018/04/gdpr-how-to-fill-in-the-information-asset-register-when-using-matomo/).

### Self-hosted
- **Security Recommendations**: Apply Matomo's [security recommendations](https://matomo.org/docs/security-how-to/) to keep data safe.
- **Hosting Contracts**: Ensure a written contract with the hosting provider guarantees [appropriate safeguards](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/guide-to-the-general-data-protection-regulation-gdpr/international-transfers/) for international transfers.
- **Data Breach Procedure**: Include Matomo in your organizational [data breach procedure](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/guide-to-the-general-data-protection-regulation-gdpr/personal-data-breaches/).
- **Data Privacy Impact Assessment (DPIA)**: Include Matomo in your [DPIA](https://www.cnil.fr/en/guidelines-dpia), if applicable.
- **Data Retention**: Configure policies for deleting raw and report data. See [[data-retention]].
