# Multi-Channel Attribution

**Summary**: Advanced reporting for Ecommerce-enabled sites to analyze how different marketing channels contribute to conversions over time.

**Sources**: [[../../raw/Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md]]

**Last updated**: 2025-05-23

---

## Overview
- **Requirement**: **Ecommerce** must be enabled for the website or app (source: Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md).
- **Navigation**: Found under **Goals > Multi Attribution**.

## Report Configuration
- **Goal Selection**: Select specific goals to view channel attribution.
- **Conversion Window**: Define how many days prior to a conversion or purchase to attribute traffic.
- **Model Comparison**: Compare various attribution models to understand different channel values (source: Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md).
