# Users Flow

**Summary**: A visual representation of the most popular paths visitors take through a website or application over several steps.

**Sources**: [[../../raw/Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md]]

**Last updated**: 2025-05-23

---

## Access and Availability
- **Availability**: Included in all **Cloud-hosted plans** or available as a purchase for **On-Premise** (source: Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md).
- **Navigation**: Found under **Behaviour > Users Flow**.

## Reports
### Visual Flow
- Displays popular pages and paths over multiple interaction steps.
- **Customization**: Controls in the top left allow adjusting detail levels and the number of actions per step.

### Top Paths
- Found under **Actions > Top Paths**.
- Provides a quick glance at the most frequent sequences of pages visited by users.

### Overview Report
- Used to investigate visitor engagement and drop-off rates.
- **Interactivity**: Clicking on a row reveals specific pages visited at that interaction step and subsequent destinations (source: Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md).
