# Real-time Reports and Visits Log

**Summary**: Tools for monitoring visitor activity as it happens and reviewing individual session histories in detail.

**Sources**: [[../../raw/Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md]]

**Last updated**: 2025-05-23

---

## Visits in Real-time
- **Navigation**: **Visitors > Real-time**.
- **Refresh Rate**: Every **5 seconds**.
- **Data Display**: New visits or page views appear at the top with a fade-in effect.
- **Visitor Attributes**: Displays date, action count, time on site, country, browser, OS, new/returning status, referrer, and goal conversions (source: Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md).

## Visits Log
- **Navigation**: **Visitor > Visits Log**.
- **Function**: Allows browsing through all visits to check individual user sessions in detail.

## Visitors Overview
- **Navigation**: **Visitors > Overview**.
- **Evolution Chart**: Visualizes trends (e.g., Visits) over time. Metrics can be changed via the line chart icon.
- **Sparklines**: Snapshot cards providing quick metrics for the selected date range (source: Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md).
