## Introduction to Matomo
- **Open-source Analytics**: Matomo is an open-source analytics service positioned as an alternative to GA3 and GA4 (source: Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md).
- **Deployment Options**:
    - **Self-hosted**: Run on your own server, free of Matomo service charges.
    - **Cloud**: Run on Matomo-rented servers (paid version).
- **Documentation Scope**: This guide covers general functionality, setup, configuration, and maintenance for both versions. It excludes deep developer-specific details (development, integration, API reference) unless relevant to major setup and functionality (source: Matomo Analytics DB 74e2f6cb8f0549379ec3cbe6c6a1a066.md).
